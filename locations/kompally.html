About this branch

The newest FitFlix in Hyderabad

FitFlix Kompally brings the full FitFlix standard to North Hyderabad — a purpose-built club on the 6th floor of Fairmount Downtown, with a dedicated strength hall, a full cardio deck, a Pilates reformer studio and expansive free-weight and functional zones.

Every floor is designed around how people actually train: natural light, generous spacing between stations, premium equipment throughout and coaches who build programmes around your goals.
