Data Safety

Account Deletion

Submit a request to permanently delete your Fitflix account and associated personal data.

Important Information Regarding Your Request

Please note that deleting your account is permanent. Once completed, your profile will be disabled, and you will lose access to all Fitflix applications and services.

What data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your profile details (full name, phone number, email address, profile avatar).
  • Your health assessment logs and compiled Fitflix Health Scores.
  • Your workout history, fitness tracking metrics, and schedules.
  • Your direct messaging logs with concierges, scientists, and trainers.

What data is legally retained:

  • Financial transaction logs, invoices, and membership payment records are securely stored as required by state and central statutory compliance, audit obligations, and tax guidelines.

Deletion Timeline:

Account deletion requests are validated and fully executed within 7 business days. We will notify you via your registered email address once the data removal process is completed.

Request Received

Your account deletion request has been submitted successfully. Our support team will process your request, and your account data will be permanently removed within 7 business days.
